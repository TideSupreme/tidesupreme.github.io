What we're obsessed with

The hard problems we get to wrestle with every day.

Ineffable Cryptography

Keys are born in pieces and never come together in one place, not on a server, not in memory, not even for us. It is breach-proof by design, with the research published for anyone to check.

TideCloak

A drop-in identity layer with an authority no one can compromise. Secure your apps and APIs in a few lines of code, and stay safe even if your own vendor gets breached.

Built in the open

Open code and self-sovereign. Run it yourself, read every line, and trust it because you can see it, not because we asked you to.

Decentralised by design

Authority lives split across a network of independent nodes (the ORKs), so no single party, breached vendor included, ever holds the keys to the kingdom.
